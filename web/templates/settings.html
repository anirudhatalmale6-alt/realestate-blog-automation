Settings

Publishing rhythm, the writer, and where posts go.

Publishing rhythm

Defaults are Tue/Wed/Thu at 09:30 and 13:00 Dubai time — mid-week, inside the UAE working week. A reasonable starting point, but not measured. Once the site has three months of analytics, swap these for the slots that actually perform.

Writer

Currently active: {{ active_provider }}. API keys are read from the environment (ANTHROPIC_API_KEY / OPENAI_API_KEY) — they are never stored in this database.

Destination

The Laravel option posts the finished article to one token-protected route in your own app — no server login, no database password, no SSH. The route is in the laravel/ folder of the repo.

Use a WordPress Application Password, not the account password — it can be revoked on its own without changing your login.

Social distribution

Built and dormant. Every publish calls the distribution hook, which fans out to the channels table. No channels are configured, so today it does nothing — adding a Facebook Page or LinkedIn channel later switches it on without touching the publish path.
